Today's Comment

US stocks slipped in a tight range on Tuesday 9/29 as long-term Treasury yields rose again. The S&P 500 fell 0.17% to 7,670.84 and the Nasdaq fell 0.09% to 26,797.54, while the 10-year yield rose 2bp on the day to 5.26%. Today's Briefing

Three key stories from trusted sources and the rest of the week's calendar

Major indexes dip slightly as the 10-year holds near a 25-year high
AP, Investrade
On Tuesday 9/29 the S&P 500 closed about 0.2% lower at 7,670.84 and the Nasdaq at 26,797.54. Rising long-term yields weighed, with the 10-year at 5.26%, near a 25-year high, while Brent crude fell 1.7% to $96.16.
Consumer confidence falls to 81.9, a 12-year low
Reuters, Conference Board
The Conference Board's September consumer confidence index dropped 6.7 points to 81.9, the lowest since April 2014 and far below the forecast of 89.2. Respondents saying jobs are hard to get rose to 21.9%, the highest since January 2021, and mentions of prices and oil rose sharply.
August job openings fall 256,000 to 7.079 million
Reuters, US Labor Department JOLTS
August job openings fell by 256,000 to 7.079 million, taking openings per unemployed worker from 1.06 to 1.01. Hires rose by 46,000, a slight improvement, but the labor market is much cooler than the 2.0 ratio of 2022.
