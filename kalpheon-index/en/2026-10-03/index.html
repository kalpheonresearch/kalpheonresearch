How to read it

Closer to 0 means fewer warning signals; closer to 100 means more. It measures where the market stands now; it is not a forecast.

0-33SafeFew warning signals
34-66CautionSome indicators show overheating or stress
67-100DangerMost indicators are red at the same time

Seven indicators (CAPE, Buffett Indicator, Fear & Greed, AAII bears, 0DTE share, VIX and the 10Y-2Y spread) are scored red = 100, yellow = 50, green = 0 and averaged. Thresholds and method are published under Methodology.
